How it works

Try on in three steps

Upload a photo, preview any outfit, and shop knowing how it will look on you.

Step 01

Upload a Photo

Add a clear full-body or upper-body photo so Vestis can understand your proportions and pose.

Step 02

Try On Any Outfit

Choose clothing from inspiration images or product photos and see an AI-generated try-on preview on you.

Step 03

Shop With Confidence

Decide with a clearer sense of fit and style before you check out—fewer returns, better choices.
