Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026. This policy explains how Vestis handles information when you use our website and upcoming app.

1. Who we are

Vestis (“Vestis,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is developing an AI-powered virtual try-on experience that helps people preview how clothing may look on them before purchasing online. This Privacy Policy describes our practices for the Vestis website and related services we operate (collectively, the “Services”).

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at thegreatestfashionapp@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to information we collect through our website, email communications, and—when available—the Vestis mobile application distributed through platforms such as the Apple App Store. It does not apply to third-party websites, apps, or services that we do not control, including retailers or platforms you may visit after using Vestis.

3. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with Vestis, we may collect the following categories of information.

Information you provide

  • Contact details, such as your email address when you reach out to us.
  • Messages and support requests you send to us.
  • Account information (if accounts become available), such as a display name or sign-in identifiers.
  • User-uploaded photos and images, including photos of yourself and clothing or outfit inspiration images you submit for virtual try-on.
  • Preferences or settings you choose within the Services.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data, such as device type, operating system, browser type, app version, language, and approximate time zone.
  • Usage data, such as pages or screens viewed, features used, and general interaction events that help us understand product performance.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance metrics, where available from the platform or our tooling.
  • IP address and related network information, which may be used for security, abuse prevention, and approximate location (for example, country or region).

Information from third parties

If you connect Vestis to a third-party service (for example, a developer integration such as Pinterest, where offered and authorized by you), we may receive information that service makes available under its terms and your settings—such as content you choose to import for try-on inspiration. We do not control those third parties’ privacy practices.

4. How we use information

We use information for purposes such as:

  • Providing, operating, and improving the Services, including generating virtual try-on previews.
  • Processing user-uploaded photos and outfit images with AI models to create try-on results.
  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support.
  • Maintaining security, preventing fraud or misuse, and enforcing our terms.
  • Analyzing aggregate usage to improve reliability, quality, and user experience.
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.
  • Communicating about product updates when you have contacted us or opted in to receive them.

Virtual try-on involves automated processing of images. Results are generative previews and may not perfectly represent fit, color, fabric, or appearance in real life.

5. AI image processing and photos

Vestis is designed to process photos you upload—typically a photo of you and one or more clothing or outfit images—to generate a virtual try-on preview.

  • We process these images to deliver the feature you request.
  • Photos you upload may be transmitted to our servers and/or trusted service providers that help us run image processing and AI generation.
  • We do not sell your photos.
  • We do not use your uploaded photos to train publicly available third-party foundation models unless we clearly disclose that practice and obtain any consent required by applicable law.
  • We may use de-identified or aggregated insights, and limited operational samples under access controls, to debug, evaluate quality, and improve Vestis features, subject to this policy and applicable law.
  • You should only upload photos you have the right to use, and you should avoid uploading sensitive images you do not want processed.

Because AI outputs can be imperfect, you remain responsible for purchase decisions based on previews.

6. Apple App Store and platform notices

When Vestis is distributed through the Apple App Store (or other app marketplaces), additional platform privacy labels, permission prompts, and system controls may apply. For example, iOS may ask you for permission before Vestis can access your photo library or camera. Those permissions are managed by your device settings. Apple and other platform providers process information according to their own policies when you use their services.

7. Pinterest and similar integrations

If Vestis offers integrations with Pinterest or similar services via their developer platforms, those integrations will only access content and account data authorized through that platform’s OAuth or permission flows. You can revoke access through the third-party service’s account settings. Content imported from those services is used to provide try-on and related features you request, and is handled under this Privacy Policy and the third party’s terms.

8. How we share information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers that help us host infrastructure, process images, send email, analyze product usage, or provide security support, under contractual obligations to protect information.
  • Legal and safety reasons, if we believe disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, where information may be transferred as part of the transaction, subject to appropriate protections.
  • With your direction, when you ask us to share information or use a feature that inherently involves sharing.

We do not sell personal information as “sale” is commonly understood (exchanging personal data for money).

9. Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy—such as providing the Services, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal requirements. Retention periods may vary based on the type of data, how you use Vestis, and operational needs. For try-on images, we aim to retain uploaded photos and generated previews only as needed to deliver the feature and support related operations, then delete or de-identify them according to our retention practices, unless a longer period is required by law or for security investigations.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use strong device protections and avoid sharing account credentials.

11. Children’s privacy

Vestis is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. International users

We may process information in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Those locations may have data protection laws that differ from the laws where you live. Where required, we will take appropriate steps to protect personal information transferred across borders.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of certain communications by following unsubscribe instructions or emailing us.

To make a request, email thegreatestfashionapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before responding. You may also control camera/photo permissions in your device settings and revoke third-party app connections in those services’ settings.

14. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are essential for basic functionality, security, or remembering preferences. If we use optional analytics cookies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide notice or choice mechanisms.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date and, when changes are material, provide additional notice as appropriate (for example, on the website or in the app). Continued use of the Services after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

16. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Vestis at thegreatestfashionapp@gmail.com.
